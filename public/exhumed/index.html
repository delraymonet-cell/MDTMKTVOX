The sentence, unearthed.
The grammar, made visible.

DIADEM LTD.: The Syntactic Atlas treats language as architecture. Literary sentences are divided into their clauses, phrases, and smaller structural components, then exhibited as finely balanced panoramic diagrams. A restricted palette, a rigid typographic grid, and a great deal of white space allow complexity to remain legible.

03 / CRITICAL APPARATUS

How to read the work.

A DETAILED BUT ACCESSIBLE GUIDE TO THE POSTERING

The quietness is the point.

LTD = LINE-TREE DIAGRAM

Begin with the sentence. At the top of each plate is a literary passage. Beneath it, a tree of fine rules divides the passage into meaningful units. The branches are not decoration. They show which words belong together and how each group contributes to the entire thought.

Read downward. The top node, S, means sentence. It can split into a noun phrase (NP), usually who or what the sentence is about, and a verb phrase (VP), what is happening. Other branches show prepositional phrases (PP), adverb phrases (AdvP), subordinate clauses (SubCl), relative clauses (RelCl), and participial phrases (PartP). The bottom level returns to the words themselves.

Notice what the syntax does, not merely what it names. Gatsby's future continually withdraws through a relative clause and successive declarations. The two questions in The Beauty of Agony place their verbs before their subjects. Death / Eternity consists of fragments, each grammatically incomplete but artistically deliberate. Structure creates pressure, pace, delay, and emotional direction.

Why the enormous white space? It lets the eye track grammatical relationships without interference. The near-monochrome palette, restrained burgundy marks, classical serif lettering, thin lines, and quiet institutional headers borrow from scholarly charts and museum print rooms. The image behaves like a specimen: austere, intimate, and exacting. There is nothing to hide behind. Typography has to do the labor.

Why the 16:9 panorama? Some sentences need room for embedded clauses, parallel phrases, and long chains of modification. A broad stage protects the line tree from collapsing into illegible knots. The 27-inch display is a visual proportion reference, not a claim that a 23.5-inch print physically measures 27 inches diagonally.

Why propose carbon pigment and cotton rag? The chosen print process and uncoated heavy rag paper prioritize deep matte blacks, texture, and long-term exhibition potential over bright, glossy spectacle. Float mounting and conservation materials are specified to keep the sheet visually separate from its frame. Actual longevity depends on verified inks, manufacture, handling and storage.

Why make only ten prints and one book? Limitation shifts the series from infinitely reproducible graphic files into a deliberately bounded physical edition. The proposed executive folio does something different: it gathers the visual grammar into a unique volume that can be opened, studied, and conserved as an object.

In plain terms: DIADEM LTD. takes sentences apart, lays their workings on a gallery wall, and asks readers to see grammar as composition. The work lives between literary criticism, information design and fine-art printmaking. The diagram is both an explanation of language and a portrait of how language thinks.

NP / NOUN PHRASEA person, thing, or idea, with its modifiers.
VP / VERB PHRASEAn action, condition, or occurrence and its complements.
