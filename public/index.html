[03] // THE ARCHIVE'S DECLARATION

The city speaks in fragments.
So do we.

MDNT MRKT VOX is the spoken extension of MDNT MRKT CONT., an independent literary continuum shaped by Monet Marcel by Delray. We archive Detroit Gothic, speculative fiction, criticism, performance, and the quieter machinery of culture.

We are interested in the stories an institution marks absent, the person a ledger fails to count, and the very old human desire to be heard.

“We archive what the world keeps trying to forget.”
