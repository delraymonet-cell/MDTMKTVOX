# MDNT MRKT CONT. merchandise

Research checked October 31, 2026. Planning assumption: a Detroit-based, primarily US launch. Supplier catalogs are evidence of available services, not confirmation of an MDNT MRKT account, configured product, inventory reservation or live checkout.

## Recommendation

Sample **Printful** first for the six apparel/accessory categories. It has a true 5.3 oz midweight tee and a coherent fleece/headwear/tote range. This recommendation favors catalog fit and a single supplier account; it does not claim independently tested quality or lowest total cost. Keep **MiiR outside automated POD** until the commercial route is confirmed.

## American Apparel and alternatives

- **American Apparel 1301GD through Printful:** 6 oz/203.4 gsm, 100% cotton, relaxed, garment-dyed, no minimum. The supplier calls it heavyweight. It is an optional vintage-style alternative, not the same cloth or fit as the classic fine-jersey tee. Blank sourced from Nicaragua. [Official listing](https://www.printful.com/custom/mens/shirts/unisex-garment-dyed-heavyweight-cotton-tee-american-apparel-1301gd).
- **American Apparel 2001:** the manufacturer guide lists 4.3 oz fine jersey; the plain 1301 is 6 oz. Neither an active Printful 2001 nor a plain 1301 listing was verified. [Manufacturer guide, pages 11–12](https://gildanstorageaccount.blob.core.windows.net/gildan-prod/assets/Pim/document/2024-GBUSA-AA-Style-and-Color-Guide.pdf).
- **Bella+Canvas 3001:** a lighter 4.2 oz option, not the closest match to a deliberate midweight brief. [Printful](https://www.printful.com/custom/products/all/unisex-staple-t-shirt-bella-canvas-3001).
- **AS Colour 5001 through Printify:** strong alternative at 5.3 oz/180 gsm, combed ring-spun cotton, US fulfillment listed. [Printify](https://printify.com/app/products/440/as-colour/adult-staple-tee). Printful's separate 5001 listing is restricted to Australia/New Zealand, so it is not the recommended US route. [Printful restriction](https://www.printful.com/custom/products/all/mens-staple-t-shirt-as-colour-5001).

## MiiR feasibility

MiiR separates custom wholesale branding from personal engraving. Most decorated products require **24 pieces or one case per design**, depending on product and technique. Its stated planning lead times are generally 12–15 business days for laser/single-color and 16–19 for multicolor after artwork approval; these are supplier estimates, not MDNT MRKT delivery promises. [Custom FAQ](https://b2b.miir.com/pages/faqs), [custom versus personal engraving](https://www.miir.com/pages/custom).

Single-unit personal engraving is not proof of arbitrary-logo fulfillment for a merchant, resale permission, automated customer shipping or an integration/API. No reviewed official source established those capabilities. Ask MiiR to confirm the chosen vessel, decoration, minimum, resale/naming terms, per-address shipping and any fulfillment partner before committing. No inquiry, order or production reservation was sent.

## Naming and artwork

Use **“MDNT MRKT CONT. — printed on [blank brand/model]”** or **“embroidered on [blank brand/model]”** only after that finished product is configured. During preview, use **“Proposed print on”** or **“Proposed embroidery on.”** A blank manufacturer's name is not evidence of a licensed collaboration. No permission for a formal “MDNT MRKT x MiiR” or “MDNT MRKT x American Apparel” partnership was established.

## Brand and implementation

The current repository overrides its earlier colored palette with Obsidian `#0A0A0A`, Bone `#F5F5F0`, Static Grey `#4A4A4A` and Ghost White. The accessible MDNT MRKT branding document supports that palette and Space Grotesk/JetBrains Mono headings with Inter body type. The new page follows those rules, sharp borders and concise literary copy. No private source-document text or unrelated business material is copied into the public page.

- New route: `/merch/`, served directly from `public/merch/index.html`.
- Page-specific styles: `public/merch/merch.css`; no build step or new JavaScript dependency.
- Added MERCH navigation on home, newsletter and reading-room pages.
- Page remains usable without JavaScript. Native disclosures expose source/specification details.
- No prices, inventory counts, purchasable variants, order forms, checkout buttons, fabricated shopping destinations or Product/Offer structured data.
- No vendor account created, credentials handled, paid service selected or goods ordered.

## Before opening orders

1. Approve slogan artwork and samples; check cotton hand, fit, wash behavior, print legibility and embroidery. Test the EU tee sizing and the hoodie fit.
2. Configure only real variants, calculate product + decoration + shipping + fees + tax obligations + returns allowance, and choose retail pricing from that model. No advertised pricing is set by this research.
3. Complete the selected storefront's account/payment setup and policies; place a test order, confirm notifications and tracking, then replace preview labels with verified product links. Keep MiiR separate unless its order and fulfillment terms are settled.
